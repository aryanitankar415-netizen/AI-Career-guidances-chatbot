ABOUT THE PROJECT

AI Career Guidance Chatbot

A student-focused web application that combines a conversational AI assistant with structured career information and learning roadmaps.

Project objective

The goal is to reduce confusion around career selection by providing accessible, personalized and actionable guidance through a chatbot interface.

  • Understand student goals
  • Suggest relevant career paths
  • Explain required skills
  • Create learning roadmaps
  • Support resume and interview preparation

Technology stack

HTML5CSS3JavaScriptPython FlaskSQLiteGemini API

Architecture

Browser → Flask routes/API → Gemini AI + SQLite → JSON/HTML response.
